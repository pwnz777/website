About

Marketing is the last business function still held together by coordination.

Design, media buying, analytics and CRM each have capable tools. What has never existed is the layer between them — where a brief becomes work, work becomes results, and results inform the next decision without anyone rekeying anything.

Mission

Give any business the operating capability that until now required a marketing department, without requiring one.

Vision

Marketing operations should be a system a business configures, not a coordination problem it manages. The tools should agree with each other by default, and the person in charge should spend their attention on decisions rather than on reconciliation.

Why the platform exists

Most businesses do not lack marketing tools. They lack a place where the tools agree. A campaign is planned in one system, built in another, measured in a third and reported in a fourth, and the work between them consumes the week. That gap is where budget quietly dies, and no individual tool can close it because the gap is not inside any of them.

The platform

ADOS connects the services a business already uses and runs operations across them from a single workspace: planning, website and content work, advertising, SEO, analytics reconciliation, CRM synchronisation and reporting. It prepares the work; a person approves what goes live.

Technology philosophy

Five positions we build against.

Authorisation is not a formality

The platform acts inside accounts that belong to you. Every permission is scoped, every action is recorded, and anything that changes what the public sees waits for a person. We built the constraint surface before the autonomy.

Explain, then recommend

A recommendation without its evidence is just an instruction. Every suggestion the platform makes shows the data it came from, so you are able to disagree with it.

One set of numbers

Most marketing disputes are really disagreements about measurement. ADOS reconciles connected sources into a single view and flags where they disagree rather than hiding it.

Reversible by default

Thirty days of agent changes can be rolled back individually or in bulk. Nothing the platform does is a one-way door, because a system you cannot undo is a system you cannot trust with real budget.

Say what is true

We describe what the product does today. Planned capabilities are labelled as planned, and certifications are not claimed until they are awarded. This is a commercial position as much as an ethical one — the buyers we want are the ones who check.
