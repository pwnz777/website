FAQ

The six questions we are asked most.

How do I connect a Google account?

Open Settings, choose Connections, then Google, and complete the consent screen using an account with administrative access to the ad account. ATNOS requests the narrowest permissions required for the features you enable and never receives your password. You can revoke access from ATNOS or from your Google account security settings at any time.

Can I stop an agent while it is working?

Yes. Each agent has a pause control that takes effect immediately, and queued actions are cancelled rather than completed. Work already prepared as a draft stays in the approval queue until you decide what to do with it.

What happens to my campaigns if I stop using ATNOS?

Campaigns and pages live in your own accounts and remain under your control. ATNOS simply stops managing them. Your workspace data stays available for export for 30 days after cancellation.

How do I export my data?

Settings, then Data export, produces a complete archive of contacts, campaign history, generated material, configuration and audit logs in JSON and CSV. The encrypted download is available for seven days.

Does ATNOS publish anything without asking?

Only within limits you configure. Anything that changes publicly visible material — publishing a page, launching a campaign — is prepared as a draft and waits for your approval. Every action is labelled as automated, awaiting approval, or confirmed by you, and recorded in the audit log.

What are the support response times?

Email support is included on every plan and replies within one business day. Organisations on an enterprise agreement have named contacts, agreed response times and a documented escalation path.
